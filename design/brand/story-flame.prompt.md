## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand production isolation. Reference: the attached logo sheet,
ONLY the large leftmost logo above '01 / STORY FLAME'. The user chose that exact
first design. Create a fresh clean digital rendering of that same emblem alone
on genuine alpha transparency. Carefully preserve its exact asymmetric
book/flame silhouette and all four sculpted filled regions: broad
upward-curling jade flame on the left, slender ivory curved page in the middle,
lower-left jade book wing, lower-right jade book wing, joining visually at a
pointed bottom. Match the original sheet's graceful proportions and curved
negative-space slits. DO NOT redesign or decorate it.

CRITICAL EDGE QUALITY: This must be clean digital logo artwork, not a cutout
produced by color-keying a dark reference image. Reconstruct the smooth filled
shapes faithfully. Every contour must be a single perfectly smooth Bezier-like
edge. Interior openings and all space outside the four filled shapes must be
completely empty and transparent, including the gaps alongside the ivory
shape. No tiny isolated pixels, cyan flecks, white speckles, stray fragments,
jagged outlines, dark matte contamination or extraction halos. No disconnected
details. Solid uniform fills only: jade #69c7a2 and ivory #f2eee4, zero gradient
or texture. The large jade flame and both page wings use the same exact jade
fill.

Output just the centered emblem on a square transparent canvas with 12 percent
transparent margins, high-resolution crisp vector-like geometry. No wordmark,
text, labels, icon tile, border, background color, checkerboard, glow, shadow,
strokes, mockup or other logos.
